Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run Spendly:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and request logs, used to keep the service working.

We do not connect to your bank accounts or collect card details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide the service to you: signing you in, storing your expenses, and showing your spending summaries and breakdowns. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and protected with reasonable security measures. Passwords are never stored in plain text. You can edit or delete your expenses at any time, and you may request deletion of your account and all associated data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of serving those files. If we add other third-party services in the future, we will update this policy to describe them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.

See also our Terms and Conditions.
